1 · Brand at a glance

Braindex is a daily AI vocabulary game — 6 words, 20 seconds each, two minutes a day. The brand is playful, warm, and confident: a hot-pink brain mascot on clean white, personal in voice, and built around a daily-habit loop like Wordle or a daily crossword.

5 · Voice & messaging

Warm, personal, and encouraging — often first-person from David, the creator. It's a small community learning together, not a corporate ed-tech product. Playful but never dumbed-down.

"Have fun & upskill together."

Core messages:

  • Master AI, one word at a time
  • 6 words. 20 seconds each. 2 minutes a day.
  • The first daily AI game
  • Score 0–1000 · live leaderboard · streaks make it stick
  • Feel less behind on AI, one tiny daily session at a time
Tone: friendly, genuine, energetic. Direct address ("you"), short punchy sentences, real gratitude, a light Irish warmth.
Avoid: jargon, hype-speak, intimidating "for engineers" framing, corporate distance. This is a personal project with a human behind it.

7 · Do & don't

Do — lead with pink as the action color, keep backgrounds clean white/off-white, let the brain mascot carry personality, keep copy personal and playful, use pink-tinted shadows.
Don't — use indigo/amber (that was a stale earlier concept), use gray shadows, crowd the mascot, write in a corporate or intimidating voice, or use pink for large text blocks (reserve it for accents and CTAs).
