A note from the creator

David Carr, creator of Braindex

I'm David. I built Braindex on my own, and I still run it on my own.

The idea is one rule: everyone gets the same questions on the same day. That's it. A score on its own is meaningless. A score you can hold up against your sister, your mate at work, or the rest of the leaderboard is worth something.

I read everything people send me and I ship updates most weeks. New questions, new games, and fixes for whatever is annoying people most. If something is broken or you have an idea, tell me.

Before this I made Pi-Squared and MovieToons. Braindex came from liking two things: a bit of competition, and a game you play once a day and then get on with your life.

Thanks for being here early.

David Carr, creator of Braindex
