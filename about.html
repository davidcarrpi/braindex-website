A note from the creator

David Carr, creator of Braindex

Hi, I'm David. Genuinely, thank you for being here. Braindex is still young, and every person who signs up, plays, shares a score or sends a note makes a real difference. We are slowly growing a small, loyal community of people who like a bit of competition first thing in the morning.

The idea is simple. Everyone in the world gets the same questions each day. That one rule is what makes it worth playing, because a score means nothing until you can compare it. You get one attempt, same as everyone, and then you find out how you did against your friends, your family and the rest of the board.

I read every piece of feedback personally, and I ship updates most weeks: new questions, new game modes, and fixes for whatever people tell me is annoying. If something feels off, or you have an idea, please do tell me. It genuinely shapes what gets built next.

A little about me for anyone who does not know me. I previously created Pi-Squared and MovieToons, and Braindex is my latest project. It came out of two things I love: learning through competition, and the daily-game habit. I wanted to put those together into something you would actually look forward to opening.

So thanks for being here early. I really do appreciate it.

David Carr, creator of Braindex
